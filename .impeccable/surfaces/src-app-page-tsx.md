# Surface: Brix Brewhouse one-page site (src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: the owners first (buy the concept), then guests on phones (hours, directions, menu in 5 s), then group/event planners.
Action: "Pour One With Us" (one CTA, one modal: Visit / Large Group / Private Event). Phone + directions are the same goal.
Proof: their own facts only (brief section 1). Unknowns visible as {{CONFIRM}}. Generated imagery labelled.
Constraints: 21+ gate; WCAG 2.2 AA; full reduced-motion; Lighthouse mobile >= 90/100/100/100; noindex concept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the firehouse. Every section is a room of one building, entered through a bay door, measured on a refractometer scale. It refuses the category default: a centered beer-photo hero over cream with three amenity cards and a stock taproom gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Brix Black ground at page scale; Pour Gold is the only light source (glow, liquid, active states); Fire Red only for the primary CTA, the gate's yes, and enamel edges. Materials as fields: real brick (mortar gaps), walnut, chalk slate, brushed stainless, caps under glass. Type: Big Shoulders Display (civic signage, huge, cropped), Fraunces italic for human moments (brief-pinned), Manrope body, JetBrains Mono as dispatch/brew-sheet data. Square corners; 1px hairlines; stencil bay numbers.

STORY: A visitor arrives at a closed door, is let in, watches the place pour, learns why it's called Brix three ways, builds a flight, finds Lindsay's caps, Eli's woodwork and Chef John's kitchen, sees what's on the run board, and leaves knowing exactly when it's open and how to get there.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas (bay door at dusk). Giant BRIX in Big Shoulders cropped by both viewport edges, mono line under it, Brix Gauge on the right edge, live status pill + "Pour One With Us" in the nav. Before that: the age gate as BAY 00 over the same closed door.

FORM: brief-pinned "The Firehouse Is Pouring Again" (concept roll skipped: direction pinned by the brief). Signature interactions: scroll-scrubbed bay-door sequence, brick-built BRIX, the flight builder pour, cap flips under a moving glare.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
